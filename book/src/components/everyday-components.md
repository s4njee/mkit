## Buttons and toggles

Use a `Button` for an action such as Save. It is a stateless builder: the application owns the result of activation. Its `on_activate` callback receives both a primary pointer click and the named keyboard action; `on_click` receives pointer clicks only. If both are set, a pointer click calls `on_click` first. Disabled and loading buttons suppress both callbacks. The visible label supplies the default accessible name; `aria_label` can override it. The gallery's Loading button shows that visual state, but has no callback attached.

An `IconButton` is the compact action version. Supply a meaningful accessible label because its icon alone does not name the action. `ToggleButton` represents a lasting pressed or unpressed choice; `ToggleGroup` represents at most one selected choice from related items. These stateful drafts emit typed value-change events. In controlled mode, the owner applies an accepted value back to the entity; in uncontrolled mode, the component updates its own value. The proposed contracts are in `registry/button/spec.md`, `registry/icon-button/spec.md`, `registry/toggle-button/spec.md`, and `registry/toggle-group/spec.md`.

All declared keyboard cases for these four E7.1 drafts pass through GPUI adapters: Enter and Space for Button, IconButton, and ToggleButton, plus six orientation-aware arrow, Home, and End cases for ToggleGroup. Focused interaction tests cover button and icon-button pointer activation; toggle-button controlled, uncontrolled, and disabled behavior; and toggle-group selection and focus movement. The toggle-group keyboard cases also check cross-axis arrow no-op, disabled suppression, and controlled requests without local value changes. These focused results do not establish the full component contracts. ToggleButton passed all 18 off/on/disabled and ToggleGroup all 12 selection/disabled manifest-driven screenshot comparisons across shadcn light, dark, and high contrast at 1×/2×; representative captures were inspected. Button passed all 18 and IconButton all 12 manifest screenshot comparisons at the same three themes and two scales. Representative captures were inspected. The Button fixture also shows six variants in three sizes, and IconButton five variants in three sizes. The rebuilt macOS gallery exposed names for its visible Buttons and `AXEnabled=false` for Loading; the 12 representative native AX assertions also passed for controls listed below. Generated accessibility cases, busy announcements, and broader platform behavior remain pending. Maintainer review of the visual baselines and contracts is still required.

## Checkbox choice

Use `Checkbox` for one labelled choice. It can also show a mixed state for a group of choices; activating a mixed checkbox requests checked. In uncontrolled mode, it updates its own checked value and emits `ChangeRequested(bool)`. In controlled mode, it emits the request and waits for the owner to call `set_checked(value, cx)`. That setter clears the mixed state and redraws without emitting another event. An enabled checkbox is one tab stop; a disabled one suppresses activation and uses an “Unavailable” description as its current accessibility fallback.

The checkbox's generated Space case passes through a GPUI adapter. A focused pointer test covers an uncontrolled toggle, mixed-to-checked behavior, a controlled request followed by owner application, and disabled suppression. Its 24/24 unchecked/checked/indeterminate/disabled manifest-driven screenshot comparisons passed across shadcn light/dark/high-contrast at 1×/2×; representative captures were inspected. Active-platform checkbox announcements and maintainer baseline review remain pending. The proposed contract is in `registry/checkbox/spec.md`.

## Other choices and task progress

Use `RadioGroup` when someone must choose one option from a labelled set. Options have stable IDs, and a controlled group asks its owner to apply a new ID. Vertical groups use Up/Down; horizontal groups use Left/Right. Movement wraps and skips disabled options, while Home/End move to the first or last enabled option. Eight generated keyboard cases pass through a GPUI adapter, including disabled, controlled, no-selection, and horizontal cases. A focused GPUI test covers pointer selection, controlled updates, and Space. Its 36/36 manifest-driven screenshots passed across vertical first/second/no-selection/disabled/controlled and horizontal first states, shadcn light/dark/high-contrast, and 1×/2×. Light selected, dark disabled, and high-contrast horizontal captures were inspected. Tab traversal, active-platform radio semantics, and maintainer visual review remain pending.

Use `Switch` for a setting that takes effect as an on or off choice. It is a stateful entity: the uncontrolled form updates itself, while the controlled form emits `ChangeRequested(bool)` and waits for the owner to apply the value. Its generated Space case passes through a GPUI adapter. A disabled switch leaves tab order and uses an “Unavailable” description fallback; accessible checked and disabled announcements still need active-platform verification. Its 18/18 off/on/disabled manifest-driven screenshot comparisons passed across the same three themes and two scales, with representative captures inspected. The disabled fixture keeps its off value; maintainer baseline review remains pending.

Use `Slider` for a bounded numeric value or an ordered pair of endpoints. The draft gives each endpoint its own tab stop, prevents the endpoints from crossing, and offers arrow, Page, Home, End, and fine-adjustment actions. In controlled mode it emits the complete requested value vector without changing the displayed value until the owner applies it through `set_value(values, cx)`. All six generated keyboard cases pass through a GPUI adapter, including controlled and disabled checks. A focused pointer test covers range endpoint selection, dragging past the track bounds, and disabled suppression. The gallery shows single, range, and disabled samples. All 30 declared screenshot comparisons passed across three themes and two scales after the fill was corrected to follow the selected value or range. Broader active-platform accessibility and maintainer baseline review still need checks. Disabled thumbs request AccessKit disabled state and use an “Unavailable” description; a native gallery capture observed `AXEnabled=false` for the disabled Slider.

Use `Progress` to display task completion. It does not accept input or emit events. The caller supplies a value and may change its bounds, or choose indeterminate mode when the amount is unknown. Determinate values clamp to valid bounds; invalid bounds fall back to 0–100. The indeterminate rendering shows a centered static segment, exposes the bounds, omits the current numeric value, and requests an “In progress” description. It has no animation or live-region/busy property, and platform announcements are unverified. All 24 zero/partial/complete/indeterminate manifest-driven screenshot comparisons passed across shadcn light, dark, and high contrast at 1×/2×; representative captures were inspected. Active-platform accessibility and maintainer baseline review remain pending. The proposed contracts are in `registry/radio-group/spec.md`, `registry/switch/spec.md`, `registry/slider/spec.md`, and `registry/progress/spec.md`.

## Theme and composition

The theme `Global` supplies semantic surface, text, border, focus, danger, accent, spacing, radius, and control-size values. `SHADCN_LIGHT` and `SHADCN_DARK` provide compact neutral palettes inspired by shadcn/ui; application code may select a different theme. Text field and text area roots use full parent width, so a card or form layout determines their available width. Their editors use themed heights (`controls.medium` for the field and `controls.large * 3` for the area). The text field uses the render spacing token for input hit testing and candidate bounds; the text area's fixed inset remains under review.

## Text input contracts

The specs at `registry/text-field/spec.md` and `registry/text-area/spec.md` describe typed `InputChanged(String)` entity events, controlled and uncontrolled modes, keyboard actions, and pending conformance cases. In controlled mode, an edit appears immediately in an optimistic local buffer and emits one full-text event. The owner calls `set_value` to accept or replace it. An equal-value owner echo preserves the caret selection, marked IME text, and undo/redo history; a different owner value replaces the buffer, moves the caret to the end, clears marked text and both history stacks, then notifies GPUI to redraw. The owner synchronization API is `set_value(value, cx)`; an equal echo is a no-op without notification, and `set_value` emits no input event. TextField passes 7/7 focused registry tests and TextArea passes 10/10, including controlled owner synchronization, clipboard behavior, and pointer drag selection; the public contract still needs maintainer review.

TextField passed all 30 declared screenshot comparisons across three themes and two scales. TextArea passed all 42 after a multiline row-layout correction. These baseline comparisons still need maintainer review. Both inputs implement GPUI text handler methods for UTF-16 selection and IME marked text. The text area accepts newlines, shapes explicit lines and soft wraps, and maps caret and pointer positions through those visual lines. It has a vertical scroll viewport and scrolls vertically to keep the caret visible during keyboard navigation. Its eight generated keyboard cases pass through a GPUI adapter, covering select-all, undo, redo, copy, cut, paste, newline insertion, and disabled suppression. Focused registry tests also cover grouped typing and IME undo, caret geometry, scroll-to-caret, and drag selection across wraps and newlines. TextArea requests AccessKit `MultilineTextInput`; native macOS inspection observed `AXTextArea` with its current value, including a disabled value. Native IME placement, live-platform drag behavior, and the full accessibility contract remain pending. Consult its spec before using it for paragraph editing.

Both editors hide the caret when unfocused or disabled. The gallery's filled and invalid samples therefore show their text without a stray caret; focus an enabled editor to see its caret.

The text field's six generated select-all, undo, redo, copy, cut, and paste keyboard cases pass through its GPUI adapter. Focused registry tests also cover disabled action and native input suppression, UTF-16 selection with IME editing, token-aligned hit testing, clipboard behavior, and pointer drag selection. Native macOS inspection observed the Workspace field with its current `AXValue`. These checks do not verify native IME candidate placement, live-platform drag or clipboard behavior, or screen-reader output.

## Selecting from lists

Use `Select` for one committed option and `MultiSelect` for a set of options. Both take a persistent label and stable option IDs. Their listbox popup uses the measured trigger in a window anchored overlay, with a theme spacing gap and viewport snap/clipping. When the measured row viewport cannot fit below and more room is available above, it flips above the trigger. GPUI `uniform_list` renders a viewport of up to eight rows from the full option set; keyboard movement scrolls the active row into view. Disabled options remain visible but cannot be chosen. Each trigger requests its committed option labels as its accessible value: Select uses one label, while MultiSelect joins selected labels in option order.

An uncontrolled selection updates inside the component. A controlled selection emits a request and waits for the owner to apply it through `set_value` or `set_values`. The single-value Select commits an enabled row and closes its popup. MultiSelect toggles a row's membership and keeps the popup open, including after a row click. When every option is disabled, MultiSelect does not open an empty active selection. Select passes five generated keyboard cases and ten registry tests, including enabled Tab traversal, disabled Tab exclusion, and forward/backward focus movement. MultiSelect passes eight registry tests and all nine generated real-GPUI keyboard cases. The Tab/Shift+Tab cases verify neighboring focus stops, popup closure, and selected-value preservation. Both components bind rebindable Tab/Shift+Tab actions to close an open popup when focus moves to the next or previous stop. Select preserves its committed value, and MultiSelect preserves its selected values. Focused GPUI geometry checks cover measured-trigger placement and above-trigger positioning near the window bottom. Their manifest-driven screenshots pass 18 Select cases (closed/open/disabled) and 24 MultiSelect cases (closed/open/disabled/all options disabled) across shadcn light, dark, and high contrast at 1× and 2×. The open fixtures use a real Enter action, and the all-disabled fixture stays closed after Down; representative captures were visually inspected. A representative native macOS assertion pass observed Select and MultiSelect as `AXPopUpButton` controls with committed label values and enabled states, including disabled fixtures. Their full accessibility contracts, broader visual coverage, and maintainer keyboard/API review remain open. The proposed contracts are in `registry/select/spec.md` and `registry/multi-select/spec.md`.

The editable `Combobox` pilot filters suggestions as someone types while keeping focus in the input. Its nine generated keyboard cases pass through a GPUI adapter, and focused tests cover controlled selection and cancellation plus enabled and disabled pointer choices. Escape restores the editing session's starting value in uncontrolled mode; a controlled owner must apply the reported rollback value. The input requests AccessKit `EditableComboBox`, name, value, expanded state, list autocomplete, and disabled state; the popup and options request listbox/option semantics, selected and disabled option state, and GPUI's active-descendant focus marker. That marker is GPUI's AccessKit focus representation, not a literal web `aria-activedescendant` relationship. The pilot still lacks popup anchoring, a popup controls relationship, live result announcements, and description/error associations. Its eight accessibility cases remain pending, with no active-platform snapshot yet. All 48 declared screenshot comparisons passed across three themes and two scales after visual corrections; maintainer baseline review remains pending. See the [editable combobox pilot](combobox-pilot.md) for the source-backed example and `registry/combobox/spec.md` for the proposed full contract.

## Menus and notices

Use `DropdownMenu` for a button-triggered command list and `ContextMenu` for commands at a host-provided context point. Each supports nested panes, disabled rows, checkable items, and a displayed shortcut label. Right and Left enter and leave submenus; Home and End move through the current pane; Enter or Space activates the active leaf; Escape requests dismissal. A checkable item emits a checked-change event and keeps the menu open. Displayed shortcuts are labels only: the host must bind the actual command. Both menus pass all five generated GPUI keyboard cases, including controlled Escape and disabled-row behavior. Two GPUI integration tests cover submenu keyboard and pointer paths, check toggling, and shortcut display; each registry component also has four passing tests. The host supplies a window-point anchor and invocation target; the menu renders an anchored surface, dismisses on an outside pointer press, and returns focus after close. Two direct GPUI host integration tests pass for both menus, including an inside click that stays open. The host still coordinates trigger clicks and nested overlays. Each menu passed 24/24 declared screenshot comparisons across closed/open/outside-dismissed/submenu states, three themes, and two scales after dark and high-contrast inactive item text corrections. Active-platform menu semantics and maintainer contract and visual review remain pending.

`Tooltip` gives each instance a unique trigger ID. A focused GPUI test confirms Tab traverses to its wrapped child without adding a wrapper stop. Its 12/12 supported focused-child and disabled screenshots matched across three themes and two scales; the focused child has a visible Global focus cue. Six visible-popup cases are declared `unsupported_headless_capture` with no baselines because delayed-hover and long-press probes produced no popup pixels in the headless renderer. GPUI 0.3.5 still cannot show the visual tooltip on keyboard focus or establish an explicit described-by relationship for the focused child. Maintainer visual review and active-platform verification remain open.

`Popover` owns a focusable outlined shadcn trigger. After trigger activation, its surface uses the measured trigger left edge with a theme `spacing.small` gap, below when it fits and above near the viewport bottom when its measured height requires it. Its first measurement frame is transparent, allowing the first visible frame to use the measured height and flip above. Five package tests pass, including GPUI checks for both placements. Its manifest-driven screenshot test passes all 18 closed/open/busy × light/dark/high-contrast × 1×/2× baseline comparisons; the three 1× open theme states were visually inspected. Active-platform accessibility and maintainer review of the trigger, placement API, and baselines remain pending.

Use `Toast` for a short notice. It requests dismissal after five seconds or on Escape. Closing and reopening starts a fresh timer; busy state pauses dismissal and leaving busy starts a fresh interval. In controlled mode it sends one dismissal request and waits for the owner to apply a state change. The open surface requests a polite status live region by default or an assertive alert when configured, with title and content in its accessible name. Four deterministic GPUI timer/state tests and three generated Escape cases pass. All 24 declared screenshot comparisons passed across three themes and two scales. Actual screen-reader announcements and maintainer baseline review remain pending.

`Dialog` and `Sheet` request their title as the accessible name of the open surface. String content keeps the surface root as its only focus stop. For interactive content, the caller supplies an element factory with `with_content` and its control handles in visual order with `focus_stops`. Opening focuses the first supplied stop, and Tab/Shift+Tab cycle through those stops. The caller must keep the list aligned with rendered, enabled controls. Each component passes five generated real-GPUI keyboard cases covering Escape, root-only Tab/Shift+Tab, and interactive two-control Tab/Shift+Tab, plus six focused tests. Dialog centers its card and Sheet bottom-aligns its panel inside a host-viewport backdrop that consumes pointer presses before they reach host controls; a GPUI test checks placement and blocking for each. Mount them at the window content root for viewport placement. Hosts still manage nested overlay ordering. Busy mode shows a token-styled Status message and blocks dismissal. Each component passes 24 manifest-driven closed/open/busy/interactive × light/dark/high-contrast × 1×/2× screenshot comparisons; the busy state is visibly distinct. Active-platform name, focus, Status announcement, and modal relationships and maintainer visual review remain pending. The proposed contracts are in `registry/dropdown-menu/spec.md`, `registry/context-menu/spec.md`, `registry/toast/spec.md`, `registry/dialog/spec.md`, and `registry/sheet/spec.md`.

## Large selectable lists

`VirtualList` shows a bounded window of a large fixed-height list through GPUI `uniform_list`. It starts with the first row active. Arrow navigation moves the active row and scrolls it into view; Space toggles its selection. A row click also activates and toggles that row. In controlled mode, a selection event is a request until the owner applies it. Its generated ArrowDown and Space keyboard cases pass, as do five package tests including controlled and uncontrolled row clicks. The root requests listbox semantics and rendered rows request option and active-descendant semantics. Its 18/18 idle/selected/empty screenshot comparisons passed across three themes and two scales. Active-platform announcements, default multi-selection semantics, and maintainer visual review remain pending. The proposed contract is in `registry/virtual-list/spec.md`.

`Tree` starts with its first root row active. Right expands a branch or enters its first child; Left collapses it or returns to its parent. Clicking a visible row makes it active. A lazy branch emits one child-load request while its children are absent; in controlled mode the owner must apply expansion before the loading row appears. Both generated ArrowDown and ArrowRight cases pass through a GPUI adapter, and six registry tests cover pointer, arrow, disclosure, and lazy behavior. A separate disclosure target toggles expansion without activating the row; controlled lazy expansion waits for owner apply and requests children once. The root and visible rows request tree and treeitem semantics. Its 18/18 collapsed/expanded/loading screenshot comparisons passed across three themes and two scales. Platform announcements and maintainer review of disclosure size, behavior, and visual baselines remain open. The proposed contract is in `registry/tree/spec.md`.

`DataTable` presents sortable headers and selectable rows in a focusable grid. Pointer clicks select a row or sort a header, and dragging a header edge resizes its column. Its active cell moves with rebindable arrows and Home/End; Enter sorts an active sortable header or toggles the active data row by default; Space toggles selection and the opt-in activation mode emits `RowActivated`. A header click also focuses its active descendant. Its generated real-GPUI keyboard suite passes 9/9 cases for four arrows, Home/End, Enter row selection, Enter header sorting, and Space row selection, with active-cell and ascending-sort assertions. Five registry tests and the examples check also pass. The manifest still has five accessibility cases pending. Its 30/30 idle/selected/sorted/active-header/active-cell screenshot comparisons passed across three themes and two scales after focus and active styling corrections. Active-platform grid semantics and maintainer API/spec and visual review remain pending. The proposed contract is in `registry/data-table/spec.md`.

## Navigation landmarks

`Tabs` uses manual activation: arrows and Home/End move focus among enabled tabs, while Enter, Space, or a click requests a new selected tab. `SegmentedControl` uses radio-group behavior: an orientation-matched arrow moves focus and selects the next enabled option. Both keep controlled selection authoritative until the owner applies the request. Their generated keyboard cases pass through GPUI adapters (Tabs 9/9, SegmentedControl 8/8), with three package tests each. SegmentedControl no longer exposes `allow_empty`; the public API change needs maintainer review. Both declared screenshot matrices passed 36/36 across six states, three themes, and two scales. Disabled-property announcements, tab-panel relationships, active-platform accessibility, and maintainer visual review remain open. Their proposed contracts are in `registry/tabs/spec.md` and `registry/segmented-control/spec.md`.

`Sidebar` presents app destinations as links inside a labelled navigation landmark. Enabled links enter normal Tab order and Enter requests navigation through a rebindable action. The application owns the destination and route change. Controlled mode waits for the owner to apply the current value; uncontrolled mode updates it. Its generated Enter case passes through a GPUI adapter, and focused tests cover pointer, controlled, uncontrolled, and disabled behavior. Its 12/12 selection/disabled screenshot comparisons passed across three themes and two scales; the selection fixture visibly selects Projects after a real click. Current-page status is requested with a description and visible marker; native `aria-current` and URL exposure need platform/API work.

`Breadcrumbs` shows linked ancestors followed by a plain-text current page. Enabled ancestors participate in normal Tab order. A click or rebindable Enter action calls the host's navigation callback with the ancestor's target; the current page and disabled links are not focusable. Its generated GPUI Enter case passes 1/1, and four GPUI interaction tests cover pointer, keyboard, disabled behavior, and focus retention when a parent rerenders the unchanged trail. A 12-case screenshot matrix passes baseline comparison for selection and disabled states in light, dark, and high contrast at 1× and 2×. Focus across changed trails, native URL and `aria-current` exposure, active-platform accessibility, and broader visuals remain pending. The proposed contracts are in `registry/sidebar/spec.md` and `registry/breadcrumbs/spec.md`.

## Layout helpers

`SplitPane` divides two regions with a keyboard and pointer resizable separator. Its generated real-GPUI keyboard suite passes 10/10 horizontal and vertical arrow, Shift fine-step, and Home/End cases, asserting exact ratios and one typed resize event per action. A focused pointer and keyboard resize test also passes. Enabled dividers enter normal Tab order and show the focus token; disabled dividers are skipped. All 30 centered, resized, vertical-centered, focused, and disabled screenshots passed comparison across three themes and two scales. Representative centered, focused, disabled, and vertical captures were inspected. Its five declared accessibility cases, active-platform verification, and maintainer contract and visual review remain pending. The proposed contract is in `registry/split-pane/spec.md`.

`ScrollArea` keeps a bounded viewport over overflowing content. Its rebindable PageDown, PageUp, End, Home, ArrowDown, and ArrowUp actions pass all six generated keyboard cases through a real-GPUI adapter, including clamping and movement from an already scrolled position. A focused viewport test also passes. All 12 idle/scrolled × three-theme × two-scale screenshots passed comparison. Its two declared accessibility cases, active-platform accessibility, and maintainer contract and visual review remain pending. The proposed contract is in `registry/scroll-area/spec.md`.

`FormLayout` aligns labels, controls, descriptions, and errors. Its default and compact modes use theme spacing tokens; compact reduces row, column, and helper-text spacing. The registry crate check and test commands passed; the dedicated screenshot test passed all 12 default/compact × three-theme × two-scale comparisons. Child controls retain their own keyboard and accessibility behavior. Label association, active-platform accessibility, and maintainer review remain open. The proposed contract is in `registry/form-layout/spec.md`.

`Separator` is a static divider with no keyboard or pointer behavior. A nondecorative instance requests the pinned GPUI bridge’s AccessKit `Splitter` role and orientation; a decorative instance has no semantic role. The registry crate check and test commands passed; the dedicated screenshot test passed all 12 horizontal/vertical × three-theme × two-scale comparisons. The active-platform meaning of that role still needs review because this separator cannot be resized; maintainer visual and contract review remain open. The proposed contract is in `registry/separator/spec.md`.
