# E2.12 evidence map — testing GPUI apps

The workspace pins `gpui-pre =0.3.5` to Zed commit [`d89e9c2124b2786a390c7a451c7488601b4da2e1`](https://github.com/zed-industries/zed/tree/d89e9c2124b2786a390c7a451c7488601b4da2e1). These chapters use compiling regions from existing example tests; `scripts/check_book.py` intentionally rejects Rust includes outside `examples/`. `mkit-harness` source is linked, not included.

It is a general testing discussion, not a defect report for the pin. The three E2.12 chapters are published under `book/src/testing/`, and all three E2.12 plan boxes are checked from compiling anchored tests and the macOS screenshot workflow. Recheck commands and screenshot provenance after example or harness changes; native screen-reader and non-macOS pixel capture remain outside this evidence.
